# Full-body recipe (historical alias: Neutral pipeline)

The overall workflow is **Kolahon Outfit Pipeline — ชุดใหม่บนโครงเดียว**.
The full-body recipe dresses the accepted Neutral body while preserving anatomy/pose and
allowing garment ease. See [current standard](outfit-pipeline/standard.md), sections 1 and 5.

[Original recipe and experiment history](archive/neutral-pipeline-history.md) is retained
for reference. It is not the authority for current rig IDs, knee positions or colour status.
